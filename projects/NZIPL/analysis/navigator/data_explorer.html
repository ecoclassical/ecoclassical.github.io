⚖ Multi-use correction. BACI counts a copper cathode as copper whatever it ends up in, so a chain’s upstream is mostly trade that never reaches the technology. The Use share column is the EXIOBASE direct use share for this (chain, HS-6 code) — the fraction of the code’s trade that plausibly serves THIS chain — and each corrected column is raw × use share, reconcilable by hand from the columns shown. Only Raw Material and Processed Material carry a measurement; for every other role the share is 1.0 by assumption — no correction applied: downstream inputs are already tech-specific (marked ≡). That is a modelling decision, not missing data. The correction reaches the raw trade columns only: CAGR (compound annual growth rate), basket shares, RCA and market share are computed on raw values, and the Q1–Q4 headline views sum the HS-6 dimension away, so they cannot be corrected in the browser.

⚠ Chain baskets overlap

A code such as 260400 (nickel ore) belongs to eight baskets and is counted at full value in each. The “all chains” total is therefore a sum of baskets, not a de-duplicated world figure.

◉ Coverage and caps

The country drill-down is capped at the top 30 exporters per chain (TOP_EXPORTERS = 30 in the slice builder), so the country selector in the HS-6 product detail and segment views lists only those 30. The four headline views Q1–Q4 are uncapped — every BACI reporter is included.

Electric vehicles (EVs) are not part of this tool — the explorer covers 10 of the 11 chains — and EVs have no EXIOBASE use shares either, so they receive no corrected figures for two independent reasons.
