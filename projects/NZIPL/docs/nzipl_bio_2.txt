Oriol Vallès Codina
Senior Research Associate

Oriol Vallès Codina is a Senior Research Associate at the Net Zero Industrial Policy Lab.
He holds a PhD in Economics from the New School for Social Research and graduate studies in 
in physics, bioinformatics, and anthropology. With an interdisciplinary profile at
the intersection of ecological macroeconomics, complexity science, and classical political
economy, his research develops macroeconomic multi-region input-output growth models to explore questions of value,
accumulation and distribution within social and ecological limits. At the lab, he builds a clean industrial policy intelligence platform mapping the evolution, structure, and geography of clean value chains worldwide.

Previously, Oriol was a postdoctoral research fellow at Leeds University
Business School and University College London, and a research consultant for the Agence
Française de Développement and the World Bank.
