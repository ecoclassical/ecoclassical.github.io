What the grid is. Four Godley–Lavoie external closures (gold, fiat, OPENFIXG, quasi-float) crossed with two regional workbooks (EU/RoW, North/South) = eight scenario-grid arms. Each arm runs twelve circular-economy scenarios (1–4, 6–13; construction 5/14 excluded). OPENFIXR and OPENFLEX are excluded from the scenario grid (documented reasons in closure_region.md). Baseline qualification records the measured debt/VA level and strict |debt/VA OLS slope| < 1e-4 gate over t71–100, with the explicit quasi-float EU exception below; a cell's scenario progress is the count of its twelve runs accepted in the status CSV.

Why the EU/RoW columns were regenerated. The maintainer authorised a Ξ-consistent enterprise-premium recalibration on 2026-10-02: EU/RoW e 0.222233 → 0.219545 (North/South stays at 0.225457). Changing e breaks the old baselines, so the four EU/RoW arms are re-cut and their 48 scenarios re-run (scenario_grid_20261002_xi_status.csv); the four North/South arms are unchanged and must come back hash-identical (the pipeline rejects any drift). One measured exception: the quasi-float EU/RoW baseline's EU debt/VA slope is −1.029e-04 (2.9% over the gate) — the debt endowment cannot move the slope and λ/tax are knife-edge, so it is documented and accepted, not chased.

Measured completion: 20261002_xi has 96/96 accepted scenarios and zero failures; all 48 North/South cache hashes match the retained archive. Verified from the status CSV and unchanged_north_south_receipt.json.
