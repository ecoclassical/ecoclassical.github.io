Scenario summary

The submitted scenario summary (Table 3 of the PDF), recomputed. Numbers are % deviations at period 90 (t + 20) against the arm's own baseline, as in the submission (R/scenarios/table3_revised.R). Two exceptions: the wage share is the % change of the share, as published; the current account is the change in % of baseline value added, because the revised baseline current account is calibrated to zero and a % change of it is meaningless. Z1 = EU or North (intervening), Z2 = RoW or South (partner). The findings text is written for the whole grid; the regime chip is that arm's.

Checks: the same method applied to the published runs reproduces every number of the submitted table (e.g. Sc1 employment −0.086, Sc13 emissions −1.563, primary material −1.273). Robustness: the signs of the findings hold under fiat and quasi-float in each setup; North/South magnitudes differ but not these signs, except Sc10, where the North expands less and the South loses far more.

Measured: in both setups the partner Z2 is the extractive region, and relative to its size the intervening region extracts about the same in each (EU 9.2% of extraction from 21.3% of output; North 30.9% from 62.7%). The intensity contrast is stronger in North/South (3.8×) than in EU/RoW (2.7×). What clearly differs is monetary rank and relative size: the EU is a small, monetarily subordinate economy facing a large resource-rich RoW; the North is a large economy issuing the key currency.
