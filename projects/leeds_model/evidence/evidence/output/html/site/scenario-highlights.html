Grid: the 96 high-finance credit runs (D-58/D-60): twelve presentation scenarios × two regional setups (EU/RoW, North/South) × four external closures (gold, fiat, quasi-float, OPENFIXG), each response at period 90 against its own arm's unshocked baseline. Scenario highlights

One row per scenario: what it does to real activity, finance, the ecological variables and distribution, and whether that changes with the closure or the regional setup. Numbers are period-90 responses against the arm's own unshocked baseline, gold closure unless stated: % deviations, except debt/GDP and the wage share (percentage points) and current account/deficit (% of baseline value added). Z1 is the intervening region (EU or North), Z2 its partner (RoW or South). Chips give the value-added regime in the order gold, fiat, quasi-float, OPENFIXG (hover for the closure). Worker disposable income moves exactly with employment in every cell, so the distributional signal comes from capitalist income and the wage share.

Compared with the 2 October grid (before the capital and high-finance recalibration): value-added responses correlate 0.92 (Z1) and 0.96 (Z2) across the 88 shared cells, with a median magnitude ratio of 1.01 and 1.13; 71 of 88 regime labels are unchanged. The financial side moved more: the median response of investment and of firm loans is about twice as large, and of the capital stock about three times. That is why the dendrograms and PCA, which weight six outcome blocks equally, look similar. The 17 label changes: sc5 Int-Wood becomes production leakage in seven configurations (it was contained in EU/RoW and dual contraction in North/South); sc8 Int-Metal in North/South under gold, fiat and quasi-float goes from dual contraction to competitive displacement; sc4 Gov-Plastics and sc9 Int-Glass in North/South under gold, fiat and quasi-float, and sc7 Int-Plastics in North/South under OPENFIXG, go from dual contraction to contained.
