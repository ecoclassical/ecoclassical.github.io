The Macroeconomic Consequences of Ecological Unequal Exchange

A two-region (EU and Rest of World), 54-industry ecological stock–flow consistent model with a multi-regional input–output structure. This page runs the model in your browser: it computes the baseline and the scenario you select, from the model's own exported initial state.

Read this before comparing with the paper. The arm selector offers four versions of the model. It opens on Revised, which corrects two sector indices in the material and waste reporting blocks; the Submitted arm reproduces the model exactly as the manuscript currently under review reports it. Between those two arms, EU material extraction differs by about 65% and the sign pattern across scenarios inverts. That difference is a corrected sector index, not a modelling choice, and a revision is in progress.
Choose a variable and press Run. A 99-period run takes a few seconds.
