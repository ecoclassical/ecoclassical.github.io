- **Ricardo description — correction of the earlier framing.** *Previously:* profit as the free part
  of the surplus, rent as the bound part, rent's rise as the analogue of the second law, the
  stationary state as heat death. *Maintainer, 2026-11-11:* no classical economic thermodynamics;
  argue that the mechanism driving degrowth is income distribution — who controls the surplus, and
  who owns natural resources. *Now:* rising differential rent paid to resource owners squeezes
  profit, the fund of accumulation; technology sets how far growth can go, distribution decides how
  the economy gets there (smooth landing at low extraction, chaotic fluctuations at high); opens
  with *are the limits to growth carved in the stone of biophysical nature, or set by
  distribution?* The grid's pop-up carries a longer version, labelled "Summary", not the
  manuscript abstract.
- **Review description** written from `conservation/qmd/isikara_mokre_review_ROPE.qmd`, which has no
