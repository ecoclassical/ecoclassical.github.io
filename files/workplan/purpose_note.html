Platform Purpose

This platform builds a durable analytical and infrastructure backbone for NZIPL's clean industrial policy research — a global trade model that tracks the movement of technologies, components, and natural resources through the global economy along the product space of clean value chains. It enables assessment of the impacts of green industrial policy, trade policy, interest rates, and quotas on trade, material flows, and aggregate economic indicators — and helps identify the best opportunities for global supply chain cooperation that benefit all countries.

What it tracks
Goods classified by sequential function — raw material, processed material, product component, process equipment — placed at upstream, midstream, or downstream positions along the clean value chain.
How it is structured
Product codes are linked to each other through a tech tree (dendrite), mapping the full input–output structure of each clean technology from extraction to final product. Four integrated datasets provide coverage across trade, firm capabilities, and ML forecasts.
Who it serves
Inputs for ongoing discussions about the rules structuring competition and cooperation between countries — particularly for the Global South — and institutional consolidation of NZIPL's clean industrial policy research agenda.
